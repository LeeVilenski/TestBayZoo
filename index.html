    Welcome to Test Bay Zoo

    The build room has been turned into a zoo for the two species that keep getting in anyway.
    • Paint Shingle Beach for seagulls and Dark Corner for moths. Fences go up on their own.
    • Seagulls need a Chip Bin. Moths need a Strip Light. Give them room or they get grumpy.
    • Guests come in through the fire exit, pay £6 and wander to exhibits. Happy animals mean a better rating and more guests.
    • Hungry seagulls with no chip bin will rob the guests.
    • The Wild Darren exhibit holds Test Bay’s IT Technician. He lives on Server Room carpet and needs a Mayonnaise Dispenser. There is only one Darren.
    • Drag to pan (right-drag while painting), scroll or pinch to zoom, arrow keys/WASD also pan. Esc returns to Inspect.
